go‑lattice — playing Go against the physics

No neural network, no opening book, and no evaluation function that wasn't derived from the model. The bot ranks moves by liberty structure — the same root weights R that the liberty‑gap plot draws — looks one move ahead to see whether the reply refutes it, and samples from a Boltzmann weight. It is weak: a club player will take it apart. It is, however, transparent, and the sliders really are coefficients in its scoring. Source and the write‑up.
